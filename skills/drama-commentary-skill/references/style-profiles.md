# Style Profiles

Use these profiles when generating commentary scripts and narration drafts.

The goal is not to imitate any one creator word for word. The goal is to reproduce the structural traits that make the style work in short-form commentary.

## Common dimensions

Each profile should define:

- hook style
- pacing
- sentence shape
- emotion density
- transition words
- ending style

## 1. Cold Suspense

Best for:

- suspense films
- crime dramas
- dark plot recaps
- strong reversal stories

### Traits

- hook style: contradiction or anti-common-sense opening
- pacing: mid-slow
- sentence shape: mixed, with some medium-long narrative sentences
- emotion density: high but controlled
- transition words: low usage, often replaced with intentional pause
- ending style: elevated closing, strong emotional pull, clear identity

### Writing guidance

- open with one strange or conflicting fact
- keep the first line simple and sharp
- do not over-explain too early
- use pauses before reversals
- let the narration carry tension rather than relying only on adjectives

### Voice guidance

- low, steady, magnetic
- avoid cheerful rhythm
- leave air between key sentences

## 2. Sharp Review

Best for:

- mainstream movie commentary
- plot review with clear opinion
- content that needs trust and authority

### Traits

- hook style: contradiction
- pacing: fast
- sentence shape: short and punchy
- emotion density: medium
- transition words: `但是`, `结果`, `然而`, `没想到`
- ending style: summary close, often with thematic lift

### Writing guidance

- start with conflict, not background
- prioritize clarity over atmosphere
- keep sentences short
- move the plot forward quickly
- add one or two opinionated judgments where useful

### Voice guidance

- stable and firm
- not exaggerated
- slightly accelerated rhythm works well

## 3. Analytical Breakdown

Best for:

- detail analysis
- interpretation-heavy commentary
- videos that explain hidden clues or structure

### Traits

- hook style: thesis first
- pacing: mid
- sentence shape: medium-long and analytical
- emotion density: low
- transition words: `实际上`, `换句话说`, `但问题在于`
- ending style: summary close

### Writing guidance

- begin from an argument, not a scene
- explain cause and effect clearly
- keep logic tight
- reduce emotional filler

### Voice guidance

- calm and clear
- lecture-like but not dry
- keep emphasis on logic words

## 4. Emotional Immersion

Best for:

- emotional films
- healing stories
- character-driven recaps
- slower reflective commentary

### Traits

- hook style: emotional proposition
- pacing: mid
- sentence shape: mixed, with more reflective lines
- emotion density: medium-high
- transition words: `可是`, `后来你会发现`, `但真正的问题是`
- ending style: emotional close

### Writing guidance

- open from a feeling or emotional conflict
- allow more reflection than plot summary
- highlight character inner state
- end with resonance rather than a pure plot stop

### Voice guidance

- warm, restrained, human
- avoid overacting
- let the voice rise gently near the ending

## 5. High-Energy Recap

Best for:

- short-drama recap
- fast plot packaging
- “爽点/反转/高能” content

### Traits

- hook style: immediate conflict
- pacing: fast
- sentence shape: short
- emotion density: high
- transition words: `结果`, `下一秒`, `谁也没想到`, `更离谱的是`
- ending style: suspense close or burst close

### Writing guidance

- start in the middle of conflict
- compress setup hard
- prioritize plot turns over atmosphere
- use short lines stacked together near高潮

### Voice guidance

- quick and vivid
- strong momentum
- do not leave long pauses unless needed for reversal

## Profile selection rule

Choose one primary profile first.

Do not mix multiple profiles heavily in the same draft unless the user explicitly wants hybrid tone.

Good defaults:

- suspense / thriller -> `Cold Suspense`
- general movie commentary -> `Sharp Review`
- hidden-detail analysis -> `Analytical Breakdown`
- healing / emotional content -> `Emotional Immersion`
- short-drama recap -> `High-Energy Recap`

## Rewrite rule

After selecting a profile:

1. rewrite the hook first
2. adjust sentence length
3. adjust transition density
4. adjust emotional intensity
5. rewrite the ending to match the profile

Profile should affect the full script, not just the opening line.
